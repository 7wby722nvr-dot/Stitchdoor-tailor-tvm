How doorstep tailoring works

STEP 1

Send your request

Describe what you want stitched. Share a photo of the style on WhatsApp. We reply with the price and a pickup time.

STEP 2

We pick up and measure

Our team collects the fabric from your door. Send your measurements, or ask for a tailor to measure you at home.

STEP 3

Stitched and delivered

Your outfit comes back finished and pressed in 2 days. Pay by UPI or cash on delivery.

Questions

How much will it cost?

Price depends on the design, fabric and lining. We send you a quote on WhatsApp before we pick anything up.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Trivandrum city, including Pattom, Kowdiar, Vazhuthacaud, Sasthamangalam, Kazhakkoottam and Technopark. Not on the list? Just tell us and we will do the necessary.

How do you take my measurements?

Send them on WhatsApp, or choose a home visit where our tailor measures you at pickup. If you have a blouse or kurta that fits well, we can copy it.

What if the fit is wrong?

We fix it free within 7 days of delivery. Extra fittings or changes that were not mentioned in your original request are charged separately, and we will tell you the price before we start.

How do I pay?

UPI or cash on delivery. For larger orders we ask for a small advance on UPI.
